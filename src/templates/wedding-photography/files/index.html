        Hi. I'm Ottilie, and I'll mostly be out of the way.

        I came to weddings from newspaper work, where you get one chance and nobody poses for you. I still work that way: quietly, close, and mostly on foot. You'll get a handful of portraits we make together and hundreds of pictures you didn't know I took.

        I shoot digital for the day and carry a medium-format film camera for the moments that deserve grain. Every gallery is edited by me, by hand, in the order things happened.
