    02 Our house

    An invitation to stay a while

    We opened Alder & Ash in a former printworks with one idea: cook everything over a real fire, and look after people the way you would at home. Chef Mara Ellison and her team light the hearth at noon and cook with whatever the farms bring in that morning.

    Dinner is unhurried. Your table is yours for the evening, the bread keeps coming, and if you'd like to try a little of everything, the kitchen is happy to choose for you.

    06 Good to know

    Before you come

    Anything else? Call us on (555) 014-2290 and we'll happily help.

    Do you take walk-ins?

    Yes. Half the bar and the chef's counter are kept for walk-ins every night, and we'll always try to fit you in the dining room too.

    Is there a dress code?

    No. Come as you are, whether that's straight from work or dressed up for the night.

    Can you cook for allergies and diets?

    Almost always. Most dishes can be made vegetarian, vegan or gluten-free. Add allergies to your booking and remind your server; our kitchen handles nuts, gluten and shellfish.

    Are children welcome?

    Very. We have high chairs and smaller plates for little ones, and early tables from 5 pm suit families best.

    Can I bring my own wine?

    Corkage is $25 a bottle, up to two bottles per table, for wines not on our list.

    What if I need to cancel?

    Just reply to your confirmation email or call us. There's no charge with 24 hours' notice; for private events, terms are in your event agreement.
