    The oven

    One oven, one fire, no shortcuts

    We light the oven at 9 every morning with seasoned oak and keep feeding it until the last pie leaves. The dough is flour, water, salt and a starter we have kept alive since we opened, folded by hand and rested for two days.

    Tomatoes are crushed in the shop every morning. Mozzarella comes from a dairy two hours north, and the basil grows in boxes on the roof in summer.

      Feeding a crowd?

      Full trays, pies by the dozen and a slice table for parties and offices. Give us a day's notice for 10 pies or more.
