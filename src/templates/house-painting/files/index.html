      No. 05 How a job runs

      Five steps. No surprises.

      1. 1

        Walkthrough

        Free, about 30 minutes. We measure, look at the surfaces, test older homes for lead and leave sample swatches on your walls.

      2. 2

        Written quote within a day

        Every room and surface on its own line, the paint we will use and a start date. Colors are locked in before we arrive.

      3. 3

        Prep day

        Floors and furniture covered, holes and cracks filled, sanded and spot primed, gaps caulked. Prep is most of a good paint job.

      4. 4

        Two finish coats

        Lines cut by hand, rolled in sections so it dries even. Rooms put back and swept at the end of every day.

      5. 5

        The blue-tape walkthrough

        You walk the job with us and mark anything with tape. We fix every piece before the final invoice and leave a labeled touch-up kit.

      No. 07 Questions

      Good to know before we start.

      Something else on your mind? Call (555) 014-2290 and talk to a painter, not a call center.

      Do I need to move my furniture?

      No. We move furniture to the middle of the room, cover it and put it back. We only ask you to take fragile things, art and electronics off the walls and shelves.

      Which paint do you use, and can I choose the brand?

      We use premium low-VOC lines from the major brands and match any color from any fan deck. If you have a favorite brand or finish, tell us and we will price it that way.

      How much is the deposit?

      Nothing for interiors under $3,000. Larger jobs and exteriors take 20% to hold the date and order materials. The rest is due after your blue-tape walkthrough, not before.

      What about rain and cold on exterior jobs?

      We watch the forecast daily and only paint when it stays above 50°F and dry for the paint to cure. If weather moves your start, you get a new date the same day.

      My house is old. What about lead paint?

      For homes built before 1978 we test first. If there is lead, our certified crew contains the work area, uses dust-free sanding and cleans to EPA standards.

      Can we stay home while you paint?

      Yes. We paint one area at a time, keep a path clear and use paints with little to no smell. Pets are fine too. We just ask that they stay out of the rooms we are working in.
