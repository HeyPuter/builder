    Good to know

    Still wondering about something? Call or text us any time we are open.

    What is the difference between gel and BIAB?

    Gel polish is a thin color coat that cures under a lamp and lasts about three weeks. BIAB (builder in a bottle) is a thicker, flexible gel layered over your natural nail to strengthen it while it grows. Choose BIAB if your nails bend, peel or break.

    How do you keep tools clean?

    Metal tools are scrubbed, disinfected and sterilized in an autoclave between every client, and opened from a sealed pouch at your table. Files, buffers and pedicure liners are single use.

    Can I come in with another salon's gel or acrylics?

    Yes. Add "Safe removal" from the Enhancements menu so we book enough time to take it off gently before we start.

    Do you take walk-ins?

    Until 5 pm on every day we are open, as long as a chair is free. Weekday mornings are the quietest. Call ahead and we will tell you the wait.

    What if my gel chips early?

    If a gel or BIAB set chips or lifts within 21 days, come back and we fix those nails for free.
