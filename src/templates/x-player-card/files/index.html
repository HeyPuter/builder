X (Twitter) Player Card

Make your own playable card

This page is a template for your own Player Card. Make it yours, publish it, and people can use it right in their X feed.

How it works

  1. Open Puter AI Builder

    Use this template to start your own card.

  2. Customize it

    Tell the builder what to put in the card: an app, a video, anything interactive.

  3. Publish

    Make the website public, then post the link on X.

X’s rules say Player Cards are meant for video and audio only. X can choose to show your card as a plain link at any time, so yours may stop working inside the post.
