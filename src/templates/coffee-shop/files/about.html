Our story

A coffee cart, a corner shop, and a lot of early mornings.

Hello from Larch StreetMorrow started in 2016 as a coffee cart outside the farmers market. Two years later we moved into the old flower shop on the corner, kept the big windows, and put a small roaster in the back room. We still roast every Tuesday, bake every morning and know most of you by your order.

Tuesdays

We roast it ourselves

We buy from a handful of farms we've worked with for years, and roast in small batches on a two-kilo roaster in the back room. Beans rest a few days, then go into the hopper and onto the shelf, dated by hand.

From 6 am

Everything is baked here

Croissant dough takes three days, so someone is always folding butter. The first bake is out at 7, the second at 11:30, and anything left after 5 is half price.

The room

Stay as long as you like

Thirty-two seats, a long bar to lean on and a patio out front. The only thing we ask is that you leave the big table free for groups at the weekend.
