    FAQ

    Questions we get on every roof

    How close is the online estimate to the real price?

    It runs the same math we do: footprint times pitch, plus waste for the shape, priced per square. Most final quotes land inside the range. What moves it is hidden until we're up there: soft decking, a third layer, skylights or a chimney that needs rebuilding. The free measure locks the number before you sign.

    Do you work with my insurance company?

    Yes, with every major carrier. We document the damage, meet your adjuster on the roof and send the final paperwork. You pay your deductible and nothing more, and we never offer to waive or hide it.

    Can you roof over my old shingles to save money?

    We don't. An overlay hides rotten decking, adds weight to the structure and voids most manufacturer warranties. Every job is a full tear-off to the deck.

    What if it rains in the middle of the job?

    We watch the radar and only open what we can dry in the same day. Underlayment goes on as the old roof comes off, so the house is never open to the sky.

    Do you offer financing?

    Yes: 12 months same as cash, or fixed-rate terms up to 10 years, subject to credit approval. Ask your estimator during the inspection and you'll have an answer before they leave.

    Are you licensed and insured?

    Licensed roofing contractor RC-20418, with $2M in general liability and workers' compensation on every crew member. We email the certificates before we start.
