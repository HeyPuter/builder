05:45 — first light

Seventy minutes before the city wakes, the room turns gold.

We built Aube on a seventh-floor roof because of one window: due east, nothing in the way. Every sunrise class is timed so the sun clears the harbour while you are standing.

Heat comes from the light, not a heater. Mats, blocks and a cup of something warm are waiting when you arrive.
